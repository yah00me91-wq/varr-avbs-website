# Privacy Policy — Varr Chronicles Backup

Varr Chronicles Backup (AVBS) is a personal-use backup application operated by its owner.

AVBS is designed to access the owner's Google Drive to store and recover encrypted backup data. Project files are encrypted before cloud storage.

Google OAuth credentials and authentication tokens are used locally for authorized access. They are not intentionally published on this website or shared with unrelated users.

The application is not offered as a public service, and backup contents are not sold or used for advertising.

Google Drive processes stored backup data according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

The owner can revoke the application's Google account access through Google Account settings.
